# Audio licence

Every file under `assets/audio/` (`sfx/*.wav` and `loops/*.wav`) is generated by the project script
`tools/audio_gen/gen.py` (with `dsp.py`, `voices.py`, `beds.py` beside it). The sounds are synthesized from noise,
filters, oscillators and FM tones with fixed random seeds. No recording, sample, sample pack, library or other
third-party audio is used, and none is derived from any third-party audio.

The generated files therefore carry no third-party rights or attribution duties. They belong to the project and
follow whatever licence the project owner chooses for the project's own assets.

Regenerate them with `py -3.12 -I tools/audio_gen/gen.py`. Verify that the committed files match the script with
`py -3.12 -I tools/audio_gen/gen.py --check`.

Do not add audio here by any other route. A new sound is a new recipe in the generator.
