# IRON STATIC: CINDERFALL - Variety milestone: the pitch

Version 0.1 (proposal), 2026-11-11. For the owner. Nothing here is built. The full design is `VARIETY_SPEC.md`; every id and number is in `VARIETY_CONTENT.md`. All numbers are first-pass and untested.

## The problem, in one paragraph

Today a run is the same run every time. The five waves are a fixed script, the upgrades are one list, and the only dice in the whole simulation move spawn points by a few centimetres. That is why the greedy bot wins 20 of 20 with 61 to 81 percent core left and seeds change the result by under 5 seconds. The fix is not "more of the same". It is five new sources of difference in every run (what attacks, what you are offered, what you find, what happens to the yard, which bosses come), each one seeded so a run can still be replayed exactly, and each one a decision the player makes with incomplete information.

## The new loop in one line

**Three pushes, fifteen waves, three bosses. Between waves Lantern Cell offers three directives and you keep one, then you choose which threat comes next. Weapons are salvage you have to hold the yard to collect. The yard itself misbehaves. You win about half the time, and never the same way twice.**

## (a) One run, beat by beat (about 17 minutes)

This player is about ten runs in and has nine unlocks, among them the LMG and the prototype pack. Kit: Standard Issue, one pistol. Seed 48211.

**Act I: THE YARD**

- **Wave 1 (0:00).** Always the same opener: skitters from the east gate, then a new sight, a stream of hand-sized **motes** pouring along the gantry line. The pistol cannot keep up with them; they burn out on the cage for 6 each. Core 97%.
- **First draft.** Three directives: HOT LOADS (+20% damage), REINFORCED CAGE (+150 core), SCAVENGER PROTOCOL (+25% Scrap). One tap to select, one to confirm. She takes HOT LOADS.
- **First route.** STANDARD: Armored Column, 21 hostiles, reward +120 Scrap. HOT: Powder Train, 47 hostiles, VOLATILE SWARM (skitters blast when they die), reward: a salvage crate. She has one pistol. She takes the crate.
- **Wave 2 (0:50).** The first **burster** walks in with a striped tank on its back. A pistol round kills it in the crowd and the blast kills four skitters, which blast too, which reach the second burster. The whole column goes up in a ripple. Then the crate lands mid-yard with a cyan flare and a ring that only fills while no machine stands near it. Three skitters go and stand on it. She fires the EMP and the pistol picks them off while they stand frozen. Ring full: SALVAGE SECURED.
- **Salvage.** A SCATTERGUN. She keeps the pistol in slot 1 and puts the scattergun in slot 2. It only fires inside 9 meters, so she swaps to it (one tap on the weapon bar) when the cage is crowded. The draft that follows offers TURRET DEPLOY (the game guarantees one early) and she takes it.
- **Wave 3.** RELAY WINDOW: for ten seconds everything she breaks pays double.
- **Wave 4.** The ticker: `priority frame deployed: plated hollow warden`. An elite, with a red ring under it. The draft after it shows a red card: **GLASS CANNON, +80% damage, core max -30%, permanently.** She is at 71%. She looks at it for a while and takes INCENDIARY TIPS.
- **Wave 5, boss: NODE 5 // LANCE PLATFORM.** It never comes to the cage. It plants 14 meters out, a red line snaps from it to the core and a tone climbs for three seconds. She fires the EMP at 2.5 seconds: charge cancelled. Nine seconds later the line is back and the EMP is still cooling. Shield. The beam takes 60 instead of 160. She kills it on the third charge. Boss crate: choose ASSAULT RIFLE or LMG. Core 58%, repaired to 73%.

**Act II: THE GANTRY LINE** (banner: CHORUS PLATING: TIER II, HP +35%)

- **Wave 6.** The LMG spins up and strays set off a fuel drum she had not noticed. Draft: FIELD WELD.
- **Wave 7.** Ticker, with a countdown bar: **"Static front on the yards. Your triggers go dark in six."** Six seconds to decide: she fires Overdrive and the Shield now, because in six seconds she cannot. Twelve seconds with no abilities. The shieldbearers' wards are down too; the LMG chews through them.
- **Wave 8.** A horn. A red hatched lane appears across the yard along the old rail line: RUNAWAY FREIGHT in 4. A dead freight car crosses the screen and takes a whole armored column and an elite with it. Pure luck, and she laughs.
- **Wave 9, the close call.** She took the HOT route for a double pick: Reclaim Line in a RAIN SQUALL (range cut by 30%). The reclaimer parks out of reach and heals everything she damages. The LMG runs dry: 3.8 seconds of reload. Core 31, 22, 12%. The VENT button locks at 10%. She hits it at 12: the core spends 8% of itself, throws eleven machines off the cage and resets their attacks. Core 4%. The LMG comes back up. She clears the wave at 4%.
- **Draft, pick two.** TWIN LINK is on the table (a prototype: the holstered gun fires too). So is FIELD WELD. She wants both and that is exactly what the hot route paid for.
- **Wave 10, boss: NODE 7 // TREMOR RIG.** Every ten seconds a red ring swells under it and it stomps: turrets offline, magazine emptied. Unless the Shield is up when it lands. She learns that on the second stomp.

**Act III: CONSOLIDATION** (TIER III)

- **Waves 11 to 14.** Everything at once: splitters breaking into shards, spitters lobbing slag from the back, two-affix elites. Her build is LMG and scattergun with TWIN LINK, incendiary rounds, two turrets and a core that welds itself: a bullet build. Another night it was the Arc Projector, EMP TETHER and EMP OVERLOAD, and the whole yard turned blue every 30 seconds.
- **Wave 15: NODE 9 // OVERCLOCKED.** `node 9 consolidating. 1 of 1 will pass.` Tonight's Hulk charges: 1.5 seconds of wind-up, then three seconds at triple speed. She saves the EMP for the charge and her last Deadfall Strike for the escorts. It dies at the cage. Core 19%. VICTORY, 16:40.
- **Results.** +274 Recovered Data, MILESTONE: NODE 7 FIRST KILL +50. "ARCHIVE: YOU CAN AFFORD SLAG MORTAR." Seed 48211, SAME SEED button. She presses RETRY instead.

## (b) The six pillars

### 1. Drafts instead of a shop

After every wave: three directives, keep one. 60 directives in six families, with rarities and tags; the draft leans toward the tags you already hold, so random offers grow into a build. Scrap pays for rerolls, a second pick from the same offer, or a core patch.

- **SHOCK ROUNDS**: every 6th shot arcs to two more enemies. With an SMG or LMG it never stops arcing.
- **AFTERSHOCK**: anything that dies stunned explodes. With EMP OVERLOAD the EMP itself starts the chain.
- **REACTIVE PLATING + CORE LASH**: the cage hurts what hits it and shoots back. A build that wants them close.
- **SCRAP ROUNDS**: +1% damage per 40 Scrap you have not spent. Every reroll now costs you firepower.
- **Corrupted directives** (red, the price printed as large as the gain): GLASS CANNON; OVERCLOCKED CORE (cooldowns -40%, each use costs core HP); SINGLE POINT (lose both turrets, the Warden hits far harder).

### 2. A real arsenal

Ten weapons that fire in different ways, two carried, swapped with one tap, found in salvage crates and levelled up by finding a duplicate. The five from the concept bar keep their tiers; five are new.

- **ARC PROJECTOR (T5)**: one crack, five machines light up. Ignores armor, tears wards, overheats.
- **SCATTERGUN (T2)**: silent until they are 9 meters out, then it shoves the front row back.
- **RAIL LANCE (T5)**: a one-second charge line, then everything on that line is hit for 320.
- **SLAG MORTAR (T4)**: lobs at the densest clump and leaves a burning pool that slows.
- **LMG (T4)**: spins up to ten rounds a second; strays hit neighbours and fuel drums; a 3.8 second reload is the price.

### 3. Longer, branching runs

15 waves in three acts, generated from threat packs and a budget, not scripted. Before each wave you choose between two threats: usually one STANDARD and one HOT (25% more enemies and a modifier, for a better reward). A boss ends each act: one of two in act I, one of two in act II, and Node 9 in one of three variants. That is 12 boss sequences before anything else varies.

- Packs: SWARM TIDE, POWDER TRAIN, ARMORED COLUMN, SLAG BATTERY, SPLIT BROOD, SHIELD WALL, RECLAIM LINE, UNDERMINE.
- Modifiers: VOLATILE SWARM, RAIN SQUALL, STATIC AIR, LOW POWER, RUSH ORDER.
- Bosses: NODE 3 // BROOD CARRIER, NODE 5 // LANCE PLATFORM, NODE 6 // AEGIS ARRAY, NODE 7 // TREMOR RIG, NODE 9 // SIEGE HULK (PLATED, FED or OVERCLOCKED).

### 4. More machines, and elites

Nine new types on top of today's three (and the Hulk), each with one job and one shape you can read from above, each with a single red optic.

- **burster**: explodes where it dies. Kill it in the crowd and the crowd goes with it.
- **mote**: tiny, cheap, dozens at once. This is how the screen gets fuller without the phone getting slower.
- **shieldbearer**: everything beside it takes 40% damage until its ward is drained or an EMP drops it.
- **reclaimer**: parks at the back and repairs what you shoot. **spitter**: lobs slag from 10 meters. **splitter**: one frame, three shards.
- **Elites** carry one or two of ten affixes: PLATED, SWIFT, VOLATILE, WARDED, MAGNETIC (pulls your fire), OVERCLOCKER, SHROUDED and more.

### 5. Chaos events

Things happen to the yard. Each has a warning you can act on: a ticker line with a countdown, its own sound, a mark on the ground.

- **DEADFALL STRIKE** (you call it, 1 to 3 per run): two seconds later 450 damage lands on the densest cluster.
- **CORE VENT** (you call it): pay 8% of the core to blast everything off the cage. Paying HP to save HP.
- **STATIC FRONT**: in six seconds your abilities go dark for twelve. So do their wards.
- **RUNAWAY FREIGHT**: a dead freight car crosses the yard and takes everything on the rail line.
- **ACCORD DROP**: a weapon crate, yours only if you keep the machines off it for 12 seconds.

### 6. Meta unlocks: the Archive

Recovered Data finally buys something: weapons that join the crate pool, directive packs that add whole build families, kits that change how a run starts, and information (boss intel, route preview). It buys **variety, not power**: there is no permanent +damage, so the game never becomes a grind toward stats and the balance stays honest.

- First unlock after run 1 or 2; everything in about 20 to 30 runs.
- Kits: BREACHER (scattergun and a turret, weaker core), GUNLINE (two turrets, weaker Warden), LONG ODDS (one random prototype and one random corrupted directive).
- DAILY RUN: one seed a day, the same for everyone, full pool, standard kit.

### How three runs of the same game differ

| | Run A | Run B | Run C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kit | Standard Issue | Breacher | Long Odds |
| Routes taken | Mostly HOT for crates | STANDARD, saving the core | HOT only when the reward is a double pick |
| Build | Bullet: LMG, TWIN LINK, incendiary, 2 turrets | Fortress: scattergun, REACTIVE PLATING, CORE LASH, SHIELD BURST | Arc: Arc Projector, EMP TETHER, EMP OVERLOAD, ARC PYLONS |
| Bosses | Lance Platform, Tremor Rig, Node 9 // Overclocked | Brood Carrier, Aegis Array, Node 9 // Fed | Lance Platform, Aegis Array, Node 9 // Plated |

## (c) What is different from today

| | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Run | 5 scripted waves, about 5 minutes | 15 generated waves in 3 acts, 15 to 20 minutes, resumable after the app is killed |
| Dice | Spawn positions only | Routes, drafts, crates, events, elites, boss picks: all seeded and replayable |
| Upgrades | Buy from 5 fixed items with Scrap, during combat | Keep 1 of 3 directives after every wave; 60 directives; Scrap buys rerolls, a second pick, core patches |
| Weapons | Pistol, then SMG for 300 Scrap | 10 weapons, 2 carried, swap by tap, found as contested salvage, 3 levels each |
| Enemies | 3 types and a boss | 12 types plus elites with 10 affixes |
| Bosses | Siege Hulk | 5 bosses; 3 per run; Node 9 is always last, in 1 of 3 variants |
| The yard | Static | 10 events and fuel drums, every one telegraphed |
| On screen | Up to 50 enemies | Up to 50 enemies plus 40 minis, chain blasts, arcs, pools, strikes |
| Recovered Data | A score | The Archive: 24 unlocks of variety, no stat power |
| Bot result | Greedy wins 100%, seeds differ by 5 s | Target: a competent bot wins 40 to 70%, greedy 10 to 40%, random under 10%, at least 8 distinct winning builds |
| Player input in combat | 3 abilities, a shop drawer | 3 abilities, weapon swap, STRIKE, VENT; no drawer. Still no aiming and no tapping on the battlefield |

## (d) The eight decisions

| # | Decision | Options | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Run length** | A: 15 waves, 3 acts of 5 (15 to 20 min). B: 18 waves, 3 acts of 6 (19 to 24 min). C: 12 waves plus an endless mode | **A** | Fourteen drafts are enough to grow a build; under 20 minutes is the upper end for a phone session even with resume; 20% less content to build and balance than B |
| D2 | **What Scrap is for** | A: nothing (pure draft). B: rerolls, a second pick, core patches, on the draft screen only. C: keep a combat shop next to the drafts | **B** | A makes kills feel worthless and removes the "do I reroll or save" tension; C keeps the predictable tree you asked to replace and keeps a drawer open during combat |
| D3 | **Weapons carried** | A: 1 (a new weapon replaces the old). B: 2, swapped by a tap. C: 2 with automatic swapping | **B** | The swap is the new skill of the combat phase (swap instead of reloading, scattergun when the cage is crowded) and it fits one thumb. C plays itself; A wastes the arsenal |
| D4 | **The word for "cards"** | A: DIRECTIVES (Lantern Cell already writes the Warden one directive: "Hold B-4"). B: PATCHES. C: lift the rule and say CARDS | **A** | The design document forbids the words Cards and Workshop (IP guardrail, section 2.4). "Directive" is already canon. The meta screen is the ARCHIVE for the same reason |
| D5 | **What the meta sells** | A: variety only. B: permanent stat upgrades. C: both | **A** | With B or C the game is balanced for nobody: new players are too weak or veterans too strong, and the scorecard cannot hold. Variety keeps every run fair and keeps the unlocks interesting |
| D6 | **How to get more on screen** | A: keep 50 enemies and add 40 instanced minis plus effects. B: raise the enemy cap to 70 or more. C: stay at 50 | **A** | The phone already hitches at 50 on MID and HIGH and every enemy is a draw call. Forty minis cost about four draw calls. B needs a rendering rewrite first; it stays possible later |
| D7 | **Resume after the app is killed** | A: none. B: restart the current wave. C: snapshot at each wave clear plus the commands since (exact resume) | **C** | A 15-minute run must survive a phone call. Between waves the world is almost empty, so the snapshot is small and safe; the tail replay puts the player back exactly where they were |
| D8 | **Daily Run in this milestone?** | A: yes, as the last task. B: later | **A, and the first thing cut** | It is nearly free once runs are seeded, and it is a strong reason to come back. It is also the easiest thing to drop if time runs short |

## (e) Risks, in plain words

1. **The phone.** It already drops frames at 50 enemies on MID and HIGH. My estimate for the new effects is about 28 more draw calls, inside the hard limit but over the target. This is measured on the real phone in the first parallel wave, before the content is built, and there is an ordered list of what to cut. Note: the phone numbers I was given are not recorded anywhere in the repository yet; the first perf task writes them down.
2. **Random can feel unfair.** A draft can miss what you need; a hot route can meet a bad event. The design has pity rules, a published mercy rule, a warning before every event, and bots that test for it. It still needs your hands on it: three playtests are gates in the plan.
3. **Readability.** Ninety bodies with blasts and beams on a phone screen is the hardest part to get right on paper. The rules are strict (one red optic per enemy, danger zones are always red hatch on the ground, no damage numbers) and there are capture tests, but only a playtest proves it.
4. **Balance is a much bigger space.** 10 weapons, 60 directives, 12 packs, 10 modifiers, 10 affixes. The answer is the bot scorecard, built early, and the fact that 34 of the 60 directives are pure data.
5. **Too many choices?** 14 drafts and 11 route picks per run. Each is two taps and untimed. If it drags, routes can drop to every second wave with a data change.
6. **Size.** About 262 agent-hours in 26 tasks, about three times the work of the original slice. The loop is playable after about 100 hours (one new weapon, the draft screen, one modifier, two new enemies, resume). Everything after that is widening, and it can be cut from the end.
7. **It replaces a game that works.** The 5-wave run stays in the test suite as a fixture (all 1,063 tests keep running on it) but leaves the shipped game. The new game is not fun-tested by anyone until the slice is in your hands. That is why the slice comes first.

## What I would cut first

The Daily Run; turret types and the third weapon slot; the cosmetic crowd outside the gates; the jammer and the borer; two weapons (cutting torch, hornet rack). Never: generated runs, drafts, two weapon slots, bursters and motes, resume, the scorecard.
